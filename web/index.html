How we chose these

What you have liked

Every destination is described by what is actually mapped there in OpenStreetMap — museums, bars, parks, monuments, beach area — and compared against the character of the places you have already visited.

Travellers like you

Item-to-item collaborative filtering finds destinations that are repeatedly visited by people whose histories resemble yours, which surfaces pairings that attributes alone would miss.

When and where you are going

Climate normals decide whether the month suits the place, country price levels match your budget, and great-circle distance keeps a short trip from sending you across the world.
